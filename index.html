Tiny Slideshow

A quiet slideshow of your photos that runs on your desktop while you work.

Get it on the Microsoft StoreSupport

Tiny Slideshow keeps a rotating set of photos on your screen while you work in other apps. Show photos from a folder on your PC, your iCloud Photos folder, OneDrive, or Google Photos. Resize the window to fit your layout, or go full screen for a proper slideshow.

Show photos from

  • This deviceAny folder on your PC. Photos are read straight from the folder.
  • iCloud PhotosReads the iCloud Photos folder that iCloud for Windows syncs to your PC. Tiny Slideshow never connects to Apple's servers.
  • OneDriveSign in with your Microsoft account the first time, then choose a folder.
  • Google PhotosSign in with Google, then pick exactly which photos to show in Google's own picker. The app only sees what you pick.

What it does

  • Stays out of the way. The window isn't forced on top of your other apps, and it won't stop your computer from going to sleep.
  • Windowed or full screen. Size it to fit your desktop layout, or fill the screen.
  • Your pace. Choose how long each photo shows (the default is 5 seconds) and turn shuffle on or off.
  • Picks up where you left off. For folders on your PC, iCloud, and OneDrive, it reopens to the source you were last showing.
  • Stays current. Add or remove photos in a folder on your PC, including your iCloud folder, and the running slideshow updates by itself.
  • Room for big libraries. Show up to 50,000 images, and filter by image type.
  • Light or dark. Match your system theme or choose one.
  • Common formats. BMP, JPG/JPEG, GIF, PNG, TIFF and ICO.

A closer look

Getting started

  1. Install Tiny Slideshow from the Microsoft Store.
  2. Set how long each photo shows.
  3. Choose your photo source and, for OneDrive or Google Photos, sign in.
  4. Resize the window where you want it, or go full screen.

Your photos stay where they are

Tiny Slideshow never stores copies of your photos. It shows them directly from the source you choose, and nothing is copied to or kept by the app.There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Read the full privacy policy.

What's new

Version 2.0

  • Added iCloud and OneDrive as photo sources. iCloud reads from the local iCloud Photos folder that iCloud for Windows syncs to your PC; OneDrive connects to your OneDrive account.
  • Raised the maximum number of images from 10,000 to 50,000. New installs now default to 50,000.
  • Tiny Slideshow now remembers the source you were last showing and reopens to it on launch, so you don't have to reselect each time.
  • Slideshows now update live for folder-based sources: add or remove photos in the folder you're showing and the slideshow reflects the change automatically.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 (version 1809) or later, on x86 or x64.
  • For iCloud Photos: iCloud for Windows installed and syncing your photos to your PC.
  • For OneDrive: a Microsoft account. For Google Photos: a Google account.
